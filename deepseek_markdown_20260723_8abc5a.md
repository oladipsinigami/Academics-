# LIS 206 Practice Quiz

## Question 1
**Type:** MCQ
**Text:** What is the primary purpose of Information Management (IM)?
**Options:**
- A) To store all data indefinitely
- B) To ensure the right information gets to the right people at the right time
- C) To create complex filing systems
- D) To replace all human decision-making
**Correct:** B

## Question 2
**Type:** MCQ
**Text:** According to Henri Fayol, which principle states that each employee should receive orders from only one superior?
**Options:**
- A) Unity of Direction
- B) Scalar Chain
- C) Unity of Command
- D) Centralization
**Correct:** C

---
## Question 3
**Type:** Essay
**Text:** Explain the concept of the Information Management life-cycle and list its six stages.
**Model Answer:** The Information Management life-cycle is the process through which information flows. The six stages are: (1) Acquisition and Creation, (2) Organization and Classification, (3) Storage and Security, (4) Distribution and Access, (5) Retention and Archive, and (6) Disposition or Destruction.

## Question 5
**Type:** Essay
**Text:** How does Library and Information Management (LIM) contribute to socio-economic development?
**Model Answer:** LIM contributes to socio-economic development by establishing digital infrastructure and e-library services in underserved communities. It bridges the digital divide, democratizes knowledge, and fosters digital literacy, which acts as a pathway to poverty alleviation.
