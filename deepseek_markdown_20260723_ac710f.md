## Question 6
**Type:** Essay
**Text:** Explain the concept of the Information Management life-cycle and list its six stages.
**Model Answer:** The Information Management life-cycle is the process through which information flows in an organization. The six stages are: (1) Acquisition and Creation – gathering data from internal and external sources; (2) Organization and Classification – indexing and cataloging so information is searchable; (3) Storage and Security – housing information securely while protecting it; (4) Distribution and Access – ensuring authorized users can retrieve it; (5) Retention and Archive – keeping information with operational or historical value; and (6) Disposition or Destruction – securely deleting data that is no longer needed.

## Question 7
**Type:** Essay
**Text:** How does Library and Information Management (LIM) contribute to socio-economic development in underserved communities?
**Model Answer:** LIM contributes to socio-economic development by establishing digital infrastructure and e-library services in underserved or rural communities. Library professionals play a direct role in bridging the digital divide, democratizing knowledge, and fostering digital literacy. By providing free access to verified information and digital skills training, LIM acts as a pathway to poverty alleviation and community empowerment.

## Question 10
**Type:** Essay
**Text:** Define the four core functions of management (Planning, Organizing, Leading, Controlling) and briefly explain how each applies to running a university library.
**Model Answer:** (1) Planning: Defining goals and strategies – e.g., setting collection development targets for the year. (2) Organizing: Determining tasks and reporting structures – e.g., assigning cataloging duties to technical services staff. (3) Leading: Motivating and guiding employees – e.g., encouraging reference librarians to adopt new AI tools. (4) Controlling: Monitoring performance and correcting deviations – e.g., checking if the circulation desk meets service level agreements and adjusting staffing if needed.
