### QUESTION 02
Type: Essay
Category: Easy
Text: (a) Differentiate technically between Narrative In-Text Citations and Parenthetical In-Text Citations. Demonstrate both using a 3-author source (Adebara, Achebe, & Ailakhu, 2022) under APA 7th Edition rules versus MLA 9th Edition rules.
(b) Explain the technical rules for handling "et al." in in-text citations across APA 7th Edition, MLA 9th Edition, and Harvard Style when a work has:
i. Two (2) authors
ii. Three (3) or more authors
Model Answer: (a) Narrative citations incorporate author names directly into sentence structure (e.g., APA 7th: Mayowa-Adebara et al. (2022) noted...; MLA 9th: Mayowa-Adebara et al. argue (145)...). Parenthetical citations enclose author names and publication year/page in parentheses at the end of the clause (e.g., APA 7th: ...digital preservation challenges (Mayowa-Adebara et al., 2022); MLA 9th: ...digital preservation challenges (Mayowa-Adebara et al. 145)).
(b) i. Two authors: Always list both authors in all in-text citations in APA 7th, MLA 9th, and Harvard (never use et al. for 2 authors).
ii. Three or more authors: APA 7th uses "et al." from the very first in-text citation (e.g., Mayowa-Adebara et al., 2022). MLA 9th uses "et al." for 3 or more authors (e.g., Mayowa-Adebara et al. 145). Harvard also abbreviates 3 or more authors to "et al." in text.

### QUESTION 04
Type: Essay
Category: Easy
Text: (a) Systematically compare the five (5) analytical and secondary branches of bibliography:
i. Analytical (Critical) Bibliography
ii. Systematic (Enumerative) Bibliography
iii. Descriptive Bibliography
iv. Historical Bibliography
v. Textual Bibliography
(b) Contrast General Bibliography (Universal, National, Trade) with Special Bibliography (Subject, Bio-bibliography, Form, Period). Provide concrete LIS examples for each.
Model Answer: (a) Systematic/Enumerative: Lists and categorizes books in a logical order by author, subject, or period. Descriptive: Detailed physical description of books as physical objects (collation, pagination, bindings). Analytical/Critical: Examines paper, typography, printing processes to trace production history. Historical: Investigates history of bookmaking, printing, paper manufacturing. Textual: Analyzes how printing/editing variations affect textual accuracy.
(b) General Bibliography lists works across broad domains without subject restrictions (e.g. National Bibliography of Nigeria, British National Bibliography). Special Bibliography focuses tightly on specific parameters (e.g., Subject Bibliography on Artificial Intelligence in Libraries, Bio-bibliography of S.R. Ranganathan).

### QUESTION 05
Type: Essay
Category: Hard
Text: (a) Define an Annotated Bibliography according to LIS 214 notes and explain its standard word count (150–200 words), structure, and core components.
(b) Explain why a bibliography must follow a strict logical arrangement (Alphabetical, Chronological, Classed/Subject, Geographical) and evaluate how each arrangement serves specific user information needs.
Model Answer: (a) An Annotated Bibliography combines standard bibliographic citations with concise evaluative summaries (annotations, typically 150–200 words) describing the scope, methodology, key findings, and utility of each work.
(b) Logical arrangement ensures efficient retrieval: Alphabetical (author/title) aids quick author lookup; Chronological traces historical development of a field over time; Classed/Subject groups related thematic materials for subject specialists; Geographical organizes regional/locational studies.
